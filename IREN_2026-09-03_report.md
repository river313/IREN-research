# IREN Limited (IREN) - FIN 439 Lab 04 Research Report

## 1. What IREN is and how it makes money

### Reported facts

IREN describes itself as a vertically integrated AI Cloud Services platform that owns the data-center, compute, and software layers used for AI training and inference. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Overview," PDF p. 109]

It earns AI Cloud Services revenue from bare-metal compute and managed cloud services for AI workloads. It also continued Bitcoin mining during FY2026, earning Bitcoin rewards and transaction-fee shares from contributed hashrate and normally liquidating the Bitcoin daily for fiat currency. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Overview," PDF p. 109]

FY2026 revenue was $707.0 million: $128.8 million from AI Cloud Services and $578.2 million from Bitcoin mining. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations," PDF p. 115]

## 2. What is going well and what is going poorly

### Reported facts - going well

Total revenue increased from $501.0 million in FY2025 to $707.0 million in FY2026. AI Cloud Services revenue increased from $16.4 million to $128.8 million, and Bitcoin mining revenue increased from $484.6 million to $578.2 million. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations," PDF p. 115]

Management attributed the AI Cloud revenue increase primarily to more customers and contracts as capacity expanded. Bitcoin-mining revenue increased with a higher average Bitcoin price and more Bitcoin mined; average operating hashrate rose from 25.7 EH/s to 36.5 EH/s. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations - Comparison of the years ended June 30, 2026 and 2025," PDF p. 116]

In the prior annual filing, management reported about 1,900 NVIDIA H100/H200 GPUs operating at June 30, 2025 and said it had procured enough additional GPUs to bring the Prince George fleet to about 10,900 GPUs by the end of calendar 2025. The FY2026 10-K does not give a directly comparable total-GPU count, so this target cannot be verified from the reviewed sources. [Source: IREN FY2025 Form 10-K, filed Aug. 28, 2025, Item 7, "Overview," PDF pp. 106-107, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884825000063/iren-20250630.htm]

### Reported facts - going poorly or creating tradeoffs

IREN reported a FY2026 net loss of $702.6 million, compared with FY2025 net income of $86.9 million. Operating income changed from positive $17.3 million in FY2025 to a $1.05 billion operating loss in FY2026. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations," PDF p. 115]

FY2026 asset impairment was $638.8 million, compared with $7.2 million in FY2025. Management stated that the FY2026 charges primarily related to Bitcoin-mining hardware and certain IT, electrical, and data-center assets affected by the move toward AI Cloud Services. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations - Impairment of assets," PDF pp. 116-117]

SG&A increased to $449.1 million from $136.5 million, while depreciation and amortization increased to $417.7 million from $181.1 million. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Results of Operations," PDF p. 115]

### My interpretation

I view the strong revenue growth and rapid AI Cloud growth as positives. The results also show that the transition is costly: revenue increased, but reported profitability weakened sharply during the investment and asset-conversion period.

## 3. Challenges, risks, and advantages

### Reported facts

AI Cloud revenue depends on contracting capacity with creditworthy customers. IREN states that customer contracts are concentrated and that losing a major customer, a customer default, or reduced capacity use could materially affect revenue and debt-service capacity. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Key Factors Affecting Our Performance - Customer demand, contracting and customer concentration," PDF pp. 110-111]

AI Cloud revenue generally begins only after compute is delivered, commissioned, placed in service, and accepted by the customer. Delays can postpone revenue and trigger delay credits, service credits, damages, or termination rights. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Delivery, commissioning, testing and customer acceptance of AI Cloud Services capacity," PDF p. 111]

IREN's growth depends on securing grid-connected power and constructing and energizing data centers. It identifies exposure to permitting, grid approvals, construction, equipment lead times, power costs, regulatory changes, and community considerations. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Power availability and data center construction," PDF p. 111]

It also identifies GPU supply, tariff, cost, delivery-timing, and technology-obsolescence risks, and states that higher equipment costs cannot always be passed through to customers. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "GPU procurement, deployment, and technology obsolescence" and "Macroeconomic conditions, tariffs and supply chain," PDF pp. 111-112]

IREN reports owning its data-center, compute, and software layers and having agreements or equivalents representing about 5 GW of total power capacity across the United States, Canada, Spain, and Asia Pacific at June 30, 2026. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Overview," PDF p. 109; "Power availability and data center construction," PDF p. 111]

In May 2026, IREN entered an NVIDIA strategic partnership intended to support deployment over time of up to 5 GW of NVIDIA DSX-aligned AI infrastructure. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Recent Developments - NVIDIA Strategic Partnership," PDF p. 109]

### My interpretation

I view IREN's vertically integrated infrastructure, power positions, NVIDIA relationship, and contracted AI opportunities as important positives. These strengths do not remove the execution, utilization, competition, or financing risks.

[Source: IREN, "IREN Delivers Horizon 1 to Microsoft and Achieves NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud Status on GB300 NVL72," Aug. 13, 2026, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344413/0/en/iren-delivers-horizon-1-to-microsoft-and-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-status-on-gb300-nvl72.html]

FY2026 operating cash flow included a $1.84 billion increase in deferred revenue from AI Cloud Services customer prepayments. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Historical Cash Flows - Operating activities," PDF p. 122]

### My interpretation

The comparison supports that AI Cloud revenue expanded and Horizon 1 reached customer acceptance. It also shows a much larger financing requirement, including equity issuance and convertible notes, which is relevant to potential dilution and shareholder-return risk.

## 5. Management outlook

### Management forward-looking statements

Management aims to substantially complete the transition of data-center capacity from Bitcoin mining to AI Cloud Services by December 31, 2026. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Overview," PDF p. 109]

In the August 13, 2026 announcement, management stated that it was targeting 480 MW gross AI Cloud capacity in 2026 and 1.2 GW gross in 2027, and that it expected to deliver Horizons 2-4 later in 2026. [Source: IREN, "IREN Delivers Horizon 1 to Microsoft and Achieves NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud Status on GB300 NVL72," Aug. 13, 2026, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344413/0/en/iren-delivers-horizon-1-to-microsoft-and-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-status-on-gb300-nvl72.html]

The August 27, 2026 FY26 results material states that Horizon 2 was commissioning and Horizons 3-4 were in late-stage construction, targeting Q4 2026 delivery. It also states targets for cumulative delivery of about 0.3 GW IT in 2026 and 0.8 GW IT in 2027. [Source: IREN, "IREN Reports FY26 Results," Aug. 27, 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm]

IREN reported $4 billion of contracted annualized run-rate revenue (ARR) for 2026 capacity and $1 billion of operating ARR as of August 26, 2026. The company states that ARR is not GAAP revenue, that it is not a substitute for GAAP revenue, and that recognized revenue may be materially lower. [Source: IREN, "IREN Reports FY26 Results," Aug. 27, 2026, assumptions and notes, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm]

Management reported $3.6 billion of investment-grade GPU financing for the Microsoft contract at 6.0% and $2.8 billion of new GPU financings for non-investment-grade customer deployments. [Source: IREN, "IREN Reports FY26 Results," Aug. 27, 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm]

### My interpretation

Horizon 1 acceptance is evidence of execution, but Horizons 2-4, future utilization, profitability, and cash generation remain important uncertainties. I do not treat contracted ARR as recognized GAAP revenue.

## 6. My decision call and what would change it

**My call is WATCH-DEFER.** I like IREN's strong revenue growth and rapid AI Cloud growth. I think the shift from Bitcoin mining toward AI Cloud could create a much larger business if management executes successfully. I view its vertically integrated infrastructure, power positions, NVIDIA relationship, and contracted AI opportunities as important positives.

However, I am not ready to initiate a buy because the transition requires enormous capital investment, IREN reported a large FY2026 net loss, and execution depends on delivering and contracting AI capacity on schedule. The company reported $13.81 billion of commitments, a $702.6 million FY2026 net loss, $4.74 billion of ordinary-share issuance proceeds, and $6.30 billion of convertible-note proceeds. [Source: IREN 10-K, Item 7, "Contractual Obligations," PDF pp. 122-123; "Results of Operations," PDF p. 115; Source: IREN, "IREN Reports FY26 Results," Aug. 27, 2026, Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm]

I want more evidence that AI Cloud growth can translate into sustainable profitability and cash generation before initiating a position. This is my judgment.

I would change from WATCH-DEFER to INITIATE-BUY if IREN delivers its AI Cloud expansion on schedule and demonstrates sustained recognized revenue growth, improving profitability, and cash generation without financing or dilution that materially weakens shareholder returns.
