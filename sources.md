# IREN Lab 04 Source List

## 1. IREN Limited, Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2025

- **Date filed:** August 28, 2025
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884825000063/iren-20250630.htm
- **Relevant information:** Provides the prior-year baseline for testing management execution. In Item 7, management reported approximately 1.9k NVIDIA H100/H200 GPUs operating at June 30, 2025; stated that 5.5k B200, 2.3k B300, and 1.2k GB300 GPUs had been procured for installation at Prince George by the end of calendar 2025; and described Horizon 1 as an up-to-50MW IT-load Childress deployment targeting energization by the end of calendar 2025. It also reported FY2025 AI Cloud Services revenue of $16.4 million and total revenue of $501.0 million. This source is relevant for comparing those plans and starting metrics with FY2026 AI Cloud growth, Horizon 1 delivery, capital needs, and profitability.

## 2. IREN, "IREN Delivers Horizon 1 to Microsoft and Achieves NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud Status on GB300 NVL72"

- **Date published:** August 13, 2026
- **URL:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/13/3344413/0/en/iren-delivers-horizon-1-to-microsoft-and-achieves-nvidia-exemplar-cloud-status-on-gb300-nvl72.html
- **Relevant information:** Official company announcement stating that Horizon 1 had been delivered to and accepted by Microsoft, and that IREN achieved NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud status after NVIDIA tested the GB300 NVL72 deployment at Horizon 1. It identifies Horizon 1 as the first of four 50MW IT-load, direct-to-chip liquid-cooled deployments for Microsoft at Childress under the five-year, $9.7 billion cloud-services contract. It also states IREN was targeting 480MW gross AI Cloud capacity in 2026 and 1.2GW gross in 2027. This source is relevant to the report's on-time-delivery, Microsoft, NVIDIA, vertical-integration, and AI-transition execution questions.

## 3. IREN, "IREN Reports FY26 Results" / FY26 Results Conference Call and supporting materials

- **Date published / held:** August 27, 2026
- **Results material URL:** https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1878848/000187884826000051/irenreportsfy26results.htm
- **Conference-call event URL:** https://iren.gcs-web.com/events/event-details/iren-fy26-results-conference-call
- **Relevant information:** The official FY26 results material reports AI Cloud Services revenue of $128.8 million in FY2026 versus $16.4 million in FY2025; $4 billion of contracted annualized run-rate revenue for 2026 capacity and $1 billion operating annualized run-rate revenue as of August 26, 2026; Horizon 2 commissioning underway and Horizons 3-4 in late-stage construction with Q4 2026 delivery targeted; and cumulative delivery targets of 0.3GW IT in 2026 and 0.8GW IT in 2027. It reports $3.6 billion of investment-grade GPU financing for the Microsoft contract at 6.0% and $2.8 billion of new GPU financings for non-investment-grade customer deployments, and discloses FY2026 proceeds of $4.74 billion from ordinary-share issuance and $6.30 billion from convertible notes. It also explains that annualized run-rate revenue is not GAAP revenue and that recognized revenue may be materially lower. This source is relevant to AI Cloud growth, delivery timing, the path to profitability and cash generation, capital spending, debt/equity financing, and dilution risk.
